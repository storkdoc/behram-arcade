HOW TO PLAY

Real chess, living pieces. Every rule applies: castling, en passant, promotion, check, mate, stalemate, threefold repetition and the 50-move rule.

Your pieces see the future. Before each of your moves the engine looks many moves ahead, and every one of your pieces reads that future from where it stands: will it die in three moves, fork the King, promote, or give its life for the Queen? That is what you see on their faces, in their hands, and hear in their voices.

Hover or tap a piece to hear its plea or advice. Pick a piece up and hover a target: your whole army reacts to the move's future, cheering, shaking their heads or covering their eyes, and a faint ghost path shows the line they foresee.

Honest counsel (on by default) marks selfish or reckless advice and tells you what it costs. Switch to raw emotions to hear only what they feel. Counsel (Enter / 👑 button) shows the move the whole army agrees on.

In two-player games you only hear your own army. The enemy's thoughts stay on their side of the board.
