SECTOR 1
THE GLEAM

BEHRAM ARCADE

CUBERTRON

Electrified rooms. Sentries that shoot back. Leave before the Gleam arrives.

HOW TO PLAY

You are a walker in a grid of electrified rooms. Sentries patrol and fire. Touching a wall or a sentry ends you. Their bolts, and theirs alone, can destroy each other, and a sentry that runs into a wall comes apart.

Leave through a lit door. The door you entered seals behind you. Clear a room for a bonus. Linger, and the Gleam — a faceless shard of light — bounces in. Nothing stops it.
