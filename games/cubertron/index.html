CUBERTRON

Paint every cube. Outshoot the drones. Don’t fall.

HOW TO PLAY

Cubertron hops the diagonals of a living pyramid. Each landing paints the cube. Clear the stage when every face matches. Some cubes need two landings, some flip off if you land again, some crumble, and some slide along the edge.

Drones hop toward you, tumble down the slope, or drop from above. Later ones unpaint cubes. Shoot them. A shield, rapid fire, slow time, and a pulse appear on the board. Fall off and a disc may carry you home. Every few stages a boss waits.
