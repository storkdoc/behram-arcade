# CUBERTRON — music credits

Background music is real recorded orchestral and band performances. Nothing here is synthesized.
Each file was re-encoded to 96 kbit/s MP3. Mars, Uranus, Saturn, and Neptune are opening excerpts with a short fade.
Sound effects are synthesized in the browser. Credits appear only on the game's credits screen.
