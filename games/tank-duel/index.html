HOW TO PLAY

Hit the other tank. Every hit is a point. The tank that gets hit spins out and is knocked back, then flickers for a moment while it recovers.

One shell in the air at a time, just like the original. Most rounds run 2:16; when time runs out, the higher score wins. If it's a tie, the round goes to overtime and the next hit wins. You can also pick first-to-5, 10 or 15.

Variations: guided missiles (turn while they fly), ricochets (bounce shots off walls; your own rebound can hit you), invisible tanks (they appear when they fire, bump or get hit), night, weather, moving walls, destructible brick, wormholes, mines, power-ups and more.
