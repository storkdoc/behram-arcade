# Exo Odyssey — music credits

Background music is real recorded orchestral and concert-band performance. Nothing here is synthesized.
Each file was re-encoded to 160 kbit/s MP3 and excerpted, with a short fade in and out.
Sound effects are synthesized in the browser. Credits also appear on the game's credits screen.

Cues: Zarathustra opens the film. Venus stays with the station and the planetary lab. Jupiter scores launches and slingshots. Mercury, Dvořák, and Jupiter shuffle in free flight and are not repeated until the others have played. The Ride of the Valkyries enters for the rescue. Playback crossfades and is not restarted by a change of scene that keeps the same cue.

The sky is procedural. No NASA or ESA image file is shipped. Planet colours are computed in the shader.
