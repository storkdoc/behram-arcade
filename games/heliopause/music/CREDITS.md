# HELIOPAUSE — music credits

All background music is real recorded performances obtained from Wikimedia Commons. Each file's
licence was checked on its Commons file page (November 2026). The recordings were re-encoded to
96 kbit/s MP3, loudness-matched, and some were trimmed (with a fade-out) to keep the download small.
They stream on demand (nothing is downloaded until a piece is about to play).

Where each piece plays: title & menus — Blue Danube, Gymnopédie, Clair de lune; building between waves —
Clair de lune, Gymnopédie, Blue Danube; waves — Mountain King, Beethoven 5; boss waves — Ride of the Valkyries;
victory — Blue Danube; defeat — Pathétique.

If the recordings cannot be played (offline, blocked, unsupported), the game falls back to its original
synthesized score. All sound effects are synthesized in the browser.
