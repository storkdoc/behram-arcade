      HOW TO LISTEN

      1. Pick a decade or search. These are the biggest Hot 100 hits of each year from 1970 to 1999, matched to an iTunes listing.
      2. Play. Space, PLAY, or gamepad A streams a 30-second preview. Nothing starts until you do. On an iPhone, the silent switch can mute it.
      3. Watch the lights. Bars, wave, ring, and sparks are drawn from the preview that is actually playing. V changes the picture. They rest when the preview pauses.
      4. Queue and favorites stay on this device. If a preview will not load, Next tries another song.
      5. iTunes. Previews are provided courtesy of iTunes and stream from Apple. Download on iTunes opens that song's store page.
