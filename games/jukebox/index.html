      HOW TO LISTEN

      1. Pick a decade. Every strip is a real hit from 1970–1999. Enter, or a tap, adds it to the queue. Nothing plays until you press Play.
      2. Play. Space, the PLAY button, or gamepad A starts the song in YouTube's own player. The picture and YouTube's controls stay visible. Full songs, not a quiz clip.
      3. Queue. Move songs up or down, or remove one. Shuffle and repeat sit with the transport.
      4. Favorites are saved only on this device. Hearts do not leave the browser.
      5. If a video will not embed, the cabinet says so and Next moves on. YouTube decides availability and any ads.
