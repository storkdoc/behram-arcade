Every tune is an original composition generated by this game in the style of its decade. No real hits, bands, lyrics or recordings.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Listen. A record drops and the groove builds: drums → bass → chords → hook.
  2. Name it. Pick the track title from 4 choices. The sooner you answer, the bigger the speed bonus (up to +150 on top of 100).
  3. Streaks. Answers in a row multiply your points: ×1.25, ×1.5 … up to ×2.
  4. 50:50. Stuck? Strike out two wrong answers for half points.
  5. 10 records a round. Beat your high score for each decade and difficulty.

Titles match their sound: a disco title sounds like disco. On Crate Digger a decoy shares the style, so learn the hooks in the Record Crate.
