Music plays from YouTube; availability and any ads are controlled by YouTube. Each clip is a short excerpt of an official artist or label video, played in YouTube's own player.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Listen. A real '70s, '80s or '90s hit starts playing in the YouTube player. The picture and title stay under a record cover until you answer.
  2. Name it. Pick the song from 4 choices. The sooner you answer, the bigger the speed bonus (up to +150 on top of 100). The clock only runs while the song is actually playing.
  3. Streaks. Answers in a row multiply your points: ×1.25, ×1.5 … up to ×2.
  4. 50:50. Stuck? Strike out two wrong answers for half points.
  5. The reveal. The cover lifts to show the video, and the song keeps playing. WATCH ON YOUTUBE opens it there.
  6. One exact excerpt per song. Each song plays the same hand-set 15-second window, picked at its chorus or signature hook. REPLAY plays that exact window again.
  7. 10 songs a round. Easy shows the artist on every choice, Normal shows titles only, Crate Digger adds a decoy from the same genre.

Music plays from YouTube; availability and any ads are controlled by YouTube. If a video won't play where you are, the game swaps in another song. If nothing starts on a phone, tap the player once.
