HOW TO PLAY

Launch: On your marks… Set… press SPACE (or TURBO) the instant you see GO! for a Rocket Start. Too early is a false start and you stumble.

Steer: ← → / A D change lanes (tap = one lane, hold = glide). The inside lane is shorter on the bends!

Draft: Tuck in right behind a runner to fill the blue DRAFT meter, then pull out for a Slingshot. Drafting also shelters you from wind gusts.

Turbo: hold SPACE to sprint (burns stamina). Hold ↓ / S to pace yourself and recover. Run out and you're gassed.

Jump: ↑ / W. Jump so you're at the top of your leap over a hurdle for a PERFECT burst. Jump puddles and mud too. Ramps launch you.

Power-ups: run through ? boxes, use with SHIFT or E: 👟 Golden Cleats (turbo rocket) · ⭐ Star Shield · 💨 Gust Wave · 🧲 Spring Magnet · 🟤 Mud Splash trap.

Touch: ◀ ▶ buttons or swipe to change lanes, swipe up or JUMP to jump, hold TURBO, tap ITEM. Gamepad: stick/d-pad steer, A jump, RT/X turbo, B/RB item, Start pause.

P / ESC pause · M mute. Top 3 win a medal on the podium!
