BEHRAM ARCADE

GLOW MUNCH

Eat the lights. Slip the four. A dash when the meter is full.

HOW TO PLAY

You are a spark in a neon maze. Eat every light. Four wisps hunt you, and each one thinks differently. Rush comes straight for you. Veil aims where you are headed. Echo steers off Rush. Loom hangs back until you are close, then peels away.

Power lamps turn the wisps. A chain of bites pays 200, then 400, 800, and 1600. Keep moving and the lights are worth more. The meter fills as you eat. Spend it on a dash. Treats appear twice a lane. Each lane is a little faster. Touch a wisp that is not turned and you lose a spark.
