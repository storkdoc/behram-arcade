    How to play

    1. Brief: Dr. Behram presents the patient.
    2. Read: review the last 20 minutes of strip. Tap or click it to mark accelerations and decelerations (pick the type). Then answer: baseline, variability, accels, decels, contractions, NICHD category.
    3. Act: the monitor goes live (time is compressed). Choose bedside actions. The fetus responds, for better or worse. Some patients surprise you.
    4. Debrief: annotated strip, what should have happened and why, cord gas, grade. Print or save it as a PDF.
