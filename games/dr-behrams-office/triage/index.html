    How to play

    1. Read: the strip fills the top half and draws the last 20 minutes (tap or Space to skip), then runs live. Drag it, or use [ ], to look back 30 minutes.
    2. Decide: one call per patient. Admit, discharge, section, stop the oxytocin, amnioinfusion, scalp stim… pick it from the list below the strip.
