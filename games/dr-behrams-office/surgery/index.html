How to operate

  • Space / tap ACTION / gamepad A: main action (cut, clamp, stitch, cauterize, place)
  • Enter / tap SPECIAL / gamepad X: special action (suction, aspirate, defog the scope, flag at time-out)
  • Mouse, finger or stick: move the instrument. Arrow keys work too.
  • 1–4 answer prompts. R radio, M sound, V voices, Esc pause.
  • Laparoscopy has the fulcrum effect: move your hand left and the instrument tip goes right.
