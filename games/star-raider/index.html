HOW TO PLAY

Vrell fleets roam the 16×8 sector galaxy and move on your starbases. When enough fleets surround a starbase (the four sectors next to it), they destroy it. Lose them all and the war is over. Destroy every fleet to win.

Open the Galactic Chart (G), pick a sector and hyperwarp (H). In the tunnel the target marker drifts on harder levels: steer to keep it centered or you arrive off course. Warping costs energy and so does everything else: throttle, shields and the attack computer.

In combat raise your shields, set a cruising throttle and fire photon torpedoes that alternate left and right. The attack computer shows θ (left/right), Φ (up/down) and range; when both tracking bars go red and the range is under 240 you have a lock and torpedoes home in. Darters dodge and dogfight, Lancers flank you then strafe, Hives carry shields and launch Darters. Shoot their plasma down or dodge it.

Hits damage your systems: Photons, Engines, Shields, Computer, Long-range scan, Radio. Yellow is damaged, red is destroyed. Fly to a starbase, line it up in the crosshair within 95 units and cut the throttle to dock: a drone refuels and repairs you.

Online two-player co-op: two wingmen share one galaxy and one chart, each in their own cockpit. A destroyed wingman gets a new ship at a starbase after 9 seconds while the other keeps fighting.

Ranks run from Galactic Fry Cook to Star Commander, each in class 5 (low) to 1 (high). The harder the level, the higher you can climb.
