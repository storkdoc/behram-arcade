HOW TO PLAY

Warheads rain down on your six cities. Put the crosshair where they are going to be and fire: your interceptor flies there and bursts into a fireball. Anything that touches the fireball explodes, and its explosion can catch others — chain them.

MIRVs split into several warheads. Bombers and satellites drop bombs (100 pts). Diamond smart bombs dodge your blasts (125 pts) — box them in. The center battery's interceptors are twice as fast; the outer ones have the same 10 each.

After each wave every unused interceptor and surviving city scores, times the wave multiplier. A bonus city every 10,000 points. Lose every city and it's THE END.

EMP (Enter / ⚡) wipes out everything in a huge radius. You start with 2 and earn one every third wave (max 3).
